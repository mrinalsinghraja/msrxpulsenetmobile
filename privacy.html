Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026  ·  MSRX PulseNet for iPhone and iPad 1.0

The short version

MSRX PulseNet collects no personal data. We run no servers for the app, and it has no account, analytics, advertising or tracking. Your readings and history stay on your device.

What the app talks to

To measure your connection, the app talks directly to public services from your device. Speed-test traffic goes to Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com). Connection checks go to public servers such as 1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8 and to well-known websites. Those services can see your IP address, as any website you visit can. The Network tools contact only the site, host or address you type. The device scan talks only to devices on your own network.

What the app reads, on your device only

  • Your connection type (Wi-Fi, cellular), mobile radio type, Low Data Mode, and the data your device has used.
  • If you allow location, only so iOS will share your Wi-Fi network name (and signal strength where iOS shares it). Your location is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • If you allow Local Network access, the devices on your home network when you tap Scan.

What is kept, and where

  • History, speed-test results, outages and data usage are saved in the app’s own storage on your device, and in a small shared area used by the app’s widgets.
  • Reports and result pictures are created on your device and go only where you choose to share them. A result picture holds no IP address and no Wi-Fi name.
  • Your settings are saved in the app’s preferences. Deleting the app removes everything it saved.

Ask

Questions are answered by Apple’s on-device foundation model. PulseNet does not use any online AI service, so your questions and answers stay on your device.

Notifications and background checks

If you allow notifications, alerts are created on your device. If Background App Refresh is on, iOS may occasionally let the app take a quick reading. Neither sends anything off the device.

No tracking

There is no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party code. Nothing is sold, rented or shared, because nothing is collected. On the App Store this app’s privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it reads or keeps, this page and its effective date will change with it.
